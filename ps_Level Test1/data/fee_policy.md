Fee Policy (Greenfield College of Engineering - sample document)
Tuition fee
The annual tuition fee must be paid before 15 July for the odd semester and before 15 December for the even semester. Fees can be paid through the college ERP portal (UPI, net banking or debit card) or by demand draft at the accounts office.

Late fee
A late fee of Rs. 100 per day is charged after the due date. The maximum late fee is Rs. 2,000 per semester. After 30 days of non-payment the student's ERP login is blocked until the dues are cleared.

Refund
If a student withdraws before the start of classes, 90% of the tuition fee is refunded. After classes begin, no refund is given except the caution deposit of Rs. 5,000, which is refunded on course completion.

Scholarships
Students with a CGPA of 9.0 or above in the previous year receive a 25% tuition fee waiver for the next year.

Hostel Rules (Greenfield College of Engineering - sample document)
In-time
All hostel residents must return to the hostel by 9.00 PM. Students attending approved evening programmes (for example the AI Fluency Training, 5.00 PM to 7.00 PM) must still be inside the hostel by 9.00 PM. Late entry requires a written permission slip signed by the warden.

Leave
To go home, a student must submit a leave form to the warden at least one day in advance. Parents are informed by SMS when the leave is approved.

Mess
Breakfast 7.00 AM to 8.30 AM, lunch 12.30 PM to 1.30 PM, dinner 7.30 PM to 9.00 PM. Mess fee is Rs. 4,500 per month and is paid along with the hostel fee.

Visitors
Visitors are allowed only in the visitors' lounge between 4.00 PM and 6.00 PM on Sundays.

Electrical appliances
Personal electric kettles, heaters and induction stoves are not allowed in rooms. A fine of Rs. 1,000 is charged and the appliance is confiscated.

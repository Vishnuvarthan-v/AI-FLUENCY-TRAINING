About
The AI Fluency Training is a short-term programme on agentic AI conducted by the Training & Placement Cell for II and III year students. Sessions run from 5.00 PM to 7.00 PM on working days. Only open-source models, tools and libraries are used.

Requirements
Each student needs a laptop with Python 3.10 or above and VS Code. Students without a GPU can use Ollama with a small model or a free Groq or Hugging Face API key.

Evaluation and certificate
Every day has a training task and an assigned task. Both must be pushed to the student's GitHub repository and the link submitted in the evaluation sheet. A certificate is issued only to students with at least 80% attendance who have submitted all tasks.

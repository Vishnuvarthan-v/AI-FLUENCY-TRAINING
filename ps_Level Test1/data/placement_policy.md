# Placement Policy (Greenfield College of Engineering - sample document)
## Eligibility
Students with a CGPA of 6.5 or above and no standing arrears are eligible for campus
placements.
Students must register on the placement portal before 31 July of their final year.
## Pre-placement training
A minimum of 90% attendance in pre-placement training is required to sit for company
drives.
## One student, one job
A student who receives an offer of 6 LPA or above is not allowed to attend further
drives.

Day 8 Task Sheet – Page 2

A student placed below 6 LPA may attend one more drive for a dream offer of at least
1.5 times the current package.

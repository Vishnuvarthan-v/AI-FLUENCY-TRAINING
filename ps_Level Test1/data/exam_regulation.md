Examination Regulations (Greenfield College of Engineering - sample document)
Attendance requirement
A student needs a minimum of 75% attendance in each course to write the end-semester examination. Students with attendance between 65% and 74% may apply for condonation by paying a condonation fee of Rs. 500 per course along with a medical or official certificate. Students below 65% attendance are not permitted to write the examination and must redo the course.

Marks
Each theory course carries 40 internal marks and 60 end-semester marks. To pass a course, a student must score at least 50% overall and at least 45% in the end-semester examination.

Revaluation
Revaluation can be applied for within 5 working days of the result announcement. The revaluation fee is Rs. 300 per paper.

Malpractice
Carrying mobile phones or smart watches into the examination hall is treated as malpractice. The paper is cancelled and the case is referred to the examination committee

Library Rules (Greenfield College of Engineering - sample document)
Borrowing
UG students can borrow up to 4 books at a time for 14 days. Books can be renewed once if no other student has reserved them.

Fines
A fine of Rs. 2 per day per book is charged for late return. Lost books must be replaced or paid for at twice the current price.

Timings
The library is open from 8.00 AM to 8.00 PM on working days and 9.00 AM to 1.00 PM on Saturdays. The digital library section provides free access to IEEE and Springer journals inside the campus network.
